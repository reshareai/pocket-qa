## Read as one piece

Your app works on your phone.

But your users have every other phone. And a bug report never shows the moment they got stuck.

Pocket QA is a usability lab in every pocket.

Builders set the tasks, the devices they need, and a reward for approved work, then publish the study.

Here it is on a Solana Seeker, from our Android APK. Testers link a devnet wallet through Mobile Wallet Adapter, then save their device profile. They pick a matching study and consent. Every session, every time.

They do the tasks, record the screen, and say what got in the way. Nothing uploads until they’ve checked it.

Builders watch what really happened and write the findings themselves, then approve, or reject with a reason.

Once approved, the reward lands in the tester’s simulated balance. No real money moves.

Separately, the escrow was tested on devnet: a two-of-three vault per study. Funding, payouts, fees and refunds: all verified. Devnet tokens have no cash value.

Good work builds a reputation, ranked by quality, never by volume.

It’s built and tested end to end, with a completed escrow pilot on devnet.

Pocket QA. Real phones, real people, real findings. Built for Seeker.
